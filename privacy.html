Glueful Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026

Glueful is a career and job-discovery application. This Privacy Policy explains how Glueful handles information when you use the application, including when you connect your Google account or Gmail account.

Information we collect

Depending on the features you use, Glueful may process your account information, profile information, job preferences, application activity, and information you choose to provide to the application.

Google and Gmail access

If you choose to connect Gmail, Glueful requests only the Google permissions needed for the Gmail features you explicitly use. Gmail data is used to provide those requested features, such as reading relevant messages for career or application-related workflows.

Glueful does not sell your Google or Gmail data. We do not use Gmail data for advertising. Gmail data is not used to develop or train generalized artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models.

How information is used

Information is used to operate Glueful, provide job-discovery and career features, maintain account security, improve application reliability, and perform actions that you request.

Sharing

Glueful does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that are necessary to operate the application, such as hosting, database, authentication, and infrastructure providers, subject to appropriate safeguards.

Data retention and deletion

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, meet legitimate operational requirements, or comply with applicable law. You may request deletion of your Glueful account and associated personal data by contacting us.

You can also revoke Glueful's Google access at any time through your Google Account security settings.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Children

Glueful is not intended for children under the age required by applicable law to independently consent to online services.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the application or applicable requirements change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions or data-deletion requests, contact the Glueful project owner through the contact information provided with the application.
